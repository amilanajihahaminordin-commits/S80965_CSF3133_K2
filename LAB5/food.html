About the Festival

🎉 Free entry for all visitors! Bring your family and friends to enjoy live cooking demos and local delicacies.

The Terengganu Street Food Festival celebrates the rich culinary heritage of Malaysia's East Coast. Over three vibrant days, local hawkers, traditional snack makers, and modern food artisans gather along the waterfront to serve authentic dishes passed down through generations.

Visitors can explore dozens of bustling stalls offering freshly grilled seafood, traditional rice dishes, and sweet kuih-muih. Alongside the food, the festival features cultural performances, busking sessions, and interactive workshops suitable for guests of all ages.
