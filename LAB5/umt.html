About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as a Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) in 1979, providing facilities for practical training and research for students and lecturers.

In February 2007, the institution was officially upgraded to a full-fledged university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier university focusing on marine science, aquatic resources, and environmental sustainability.
